How it works

One booking, start to finish.

The same five things you already do for every guest — just in one place instead of four apps and a notebook.

  1. 01

    An enquiry comes in

    WhatsApp, Instagram, a phone call, a friend of a friend. Log it with the source so you know months later which one actually brings you guests.

  2. 02

    It becomes a booking

    Convert the enquiry in two taps. Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and MakeMyTrip bookings arrive on their own via calendar sync and email import.

  3. 03

    Money gets collected

    Advance, balance, cash, UPI, bank transfer, OTA payout. ResIQ tracks what's come in, what's still owed, and who to chase — and sends the reminder for you.

  4. 04

    The guest checks in

    They scan a QR code, upload Aadhaar or passport, take a selfie, pick their meals. You approve it from your phone. No register, no photocopies.

  5. 05

    And you stay in touch

    Confirmation on booking, directions before arrival, a thank-you after checkout. Templated in English or Hindi, sent over WhatsApp, logged against the booking.

Questions

The things people ask before signing up.

If yours isn't here, email support@hostizzy.com and a human will answer.

What exactly is ResIQ?

ResIQ is an app for people who own a homestay, villa or farmstay in India. It keeps your bookings, payments, guest IDs and guest messages in one place instead of spread across a notebook, WhatsApp and a spreadsheet. It is deliberately not a big hotel system — there is no 400-setting configuration screen to get through before it is useful.

Who is it for — and who is it not for?

It is for owners — you have a homestay, a villa or a farmstay, you handle the bookings yourself, and you probably have a caretaker on site. If you are a property management company running other people's places, ResIQ is not aimed at you; Hostizzy is building something separate for that. ResIQ was built inside Hostizzy, which manages 45 properties across 15 Indian cities, so it is proven at scale — but it is priced and shaped for one owner.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. You can create and edit bookings, record payments and check guests in with no network connection at all. Each action queues locally and syncs automatically once signal returns — nothing is lost and nothing needs re-entering. This matters because most properties worth staying at are in hills, farmland or backwaters where coverage is unreliable.

Which booking channels does it sync with?

ResIQ reads the calendar feeds from Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip and Goibibo, so a booking made on any of them blocks those dates in ResIQ automatically. It also reads booking confirmation emails directly from Gmail, so a reservation usually lands in your system before you've finished reading the notification. Today that sync is one-way, into ResIQ. Two-way sync — pushing your own availability back out to the channels — is in testing and lands shortly.

Will ResIQ get me more bookings?

No, and it doesn't pretend to. ResIQ is not a listing site and not a booking engine — guests can't find or book you through it. It manages the bookings you already get from Airbnb, MakeMyTrip, your own website or a WhatsApp message from someone's cousin. What it does do is stop you losing money on the ones you already have: forgotten balances, double bookings, and guests who checked out without ever giving you an ID.

How does guest KYC work?

Guests scan a QR code and complete verification on their own phone before arrival — they upload an Aadhaar or passport, take a selfie, and pick meal preferences. You review and approve from your phone. Uploaded documents are automatically deleted 60 days after checkout, so you're not holding a pile of ID scans indefinitely.

What does it actually cost?

Your first property is free, permanently — not a trial, and no card required. If you add more properties later there is a paid plan, and we will tell you the price before anything changes. Accounts are reviewed by our team before activation, usually the same day.

I only have one property. Isn't this overkill?

That is exactly who it is built for, which is why one property is free. With a single homestay the problem is not volume — it is that the booking is in WhatsApp, the advance is in a UPI notification, the guest's ID is in your gallery, and the calendar is in your head. One double booking or one forgotten balance costs more than the software does.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes — a native Android app on Google Play with push notifications, offline mode and biometric login. ResIQ also runs in any browser and can be installed as a web app on iOS, Windows, Mac or Chrome OS without going through an app store.

Can my caretaker use it too?

Yes, and they see only what they need. Your caretaker gets arrivals, departures and the ability to check guests in — but not your revenue, payouts or bank details. You invite them by email and can revoke access in one tap. Guests get a separate QR-code portal for check-in and never see the app itself.
