How it works

One booking, start to finish.

The same five steps your team already does every day — just in one place instead of four apps and a notebook.

  1. 01

    An enquiry comes in

    WhatsApp, Instagram, a phone call, a walk-in. Log it with the source so you know months later which channel actually brings you guests.

  2. 02

    It becomes a booking

    Convert the enquiry in two taps. Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and MakeMyTrip bookings arrive on their own via calendar sync and email import.

  3. 03

    Money gets collected

    Advance, balance, cash, UPI, bank transfer, OTA payout. ResIQ tracks what's in, what's pending, and who to chase — and sends the reminder for you.

  4. 04

    The guest checks in

    They scan a QR code, upload Aadhaar or passport, take a selfie, pick their meals. You approve it from your phone. No register, no photocopies.

  5. 05

    And you stay in touch

    Confirmation on booking, directions before arrival, a thank-you after checkout. Templated in English or Hindi, sent over WhatsApp, logged against the booking.

What's inside

Built for the person actually running the place.

Not a 400-setting enterprise PMS. The things you touch every day, and nothing you don't.

Every channel on one calendar

Two-way iCal sync keeps your OTA calendars honest, and ResIQ reads booking confirmation emails straight out of Gmail — so an Airbnb reservation is in your system before you've finished reading the notification.

Airbnb Booking.com Agoda MakeMyTrip Goibibo

Payments that add up

Track advances and balances across cash, UPI, gateway and bank transfer. Commission, GST and owner payouts calculate themselves, so month-end is a review rather than a reconstruction.

Guest KYC without the paperwork

Guests upload ID and a selfie through a QR portal before they arrive. You approve from your phone. Documents auto-delete 60 days after checkout, so you're not sitting on a pile of Aadhaar scans.

WhatsApp, templated

Confirmations, directions, payment nudges and thank-yous — in English or Hindi, logged against the booking.

Numbers you'll actually read

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and P&L per property. Which channel earns, which month drags, what next month looks like.

Your team, scoped

Caretakers, managers and owners each see their own slice. Invite them by email; revoke in one tap.
