Delete Your ResIQ Account

App: ResIQ by Hostizzy · Last updated: June 8, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your ResIQ by Hostizzy account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@hostizzy.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account", or ask your organisation's ResIQ administrator to remove your account.
  2. We verify your identity (to protect your data) and confirm the request.
  3. Your account and associated personal data are deleted within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your profile and contact details (name, email address, phone number)
  • Login credentials
  • Profile photo (if any)
  • Personal data linked to your user account

What may be retained

  • Transaction and booking records that we are legally required to keep for tax, accounting and audit purposes — retained for up to 7 years, in limited or anonymised form where possible.
  • Guest KYC documents are automatically deleted 60 days after checkout, independent of account deletion.
